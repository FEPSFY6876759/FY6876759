Abstract

With AI's pervasive nature in the modern day, it is only suitable to theorize how it may be expanded upon in the future. This paper explores the integration of Quantum Computing (QC) with Artificial Intelligence (AI), focusing on the basics of QC, like superposition and entanglement, and how they can enhance AI's applications. This is a brief overview of methods offering transformative solutions to complex problems in computer science.

Introduction

The intersection of Quantum Computing (QC) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) combines fields that promise to revolutionize data processing. By leveraging subatomic behaviors, QC enables groundbreaking enhancements to AI. This paper explores fundamental QC principles, quantum algorithms like Grover’s, and their transformative impact on AI.

The Fundamentals of Computing and Quantum Computing

Traditional computing uses binary states (0 or 1) as the basis of information storage. QC, however, employs qubits that can exist in a state of superposition, allowing them to represent both 0 and 1 simultaneously. This, along with entanglement and quantum interference, forms the foundation for QC's potential to surpass classical computing limitations.
